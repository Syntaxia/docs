Pillars are the top-level workspaces in Syntaxia. Each one groups the consoles a particular team works in, and each member is granted the pillars they need. The pillar you are in decides which consoles appear in the navigation and which actions are available on the pages you open.

Every organization has the same five pillars. The names below are the product defaults. See [Rename a pillar](#rename-a-pillar) for how your organization can change three of them.

| Default name | What it is for | Consoles |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue Control Tower | The landing surface every member shares. It looks across every pillar rather than owning one domain. | Cross-pillar overview |
| Data | Source management, processing, the golden layer, industries, and Vantages. The workspace for data teams and the pillar that owns workspace configuration. | Command Center, Accounts, Pipeline, Source Management, Vantages, Golden Layer, Processing, Industries |
| Planning | Planning views over the reconciled data. | Command Center |
| Operations | Day-to-day go-to-market operations: accounts, contacts, and pipeline. | Command Center, Accounts & Contacts, Pipeline |
| Finance | Reserved for finance views. It can be granted but is not on the pillar switcher yet. | Command Center |

## Switch pillars

Click the pillar name in the app header to open the switcher. The Revenue Control Tower sits above a rule, and the pillars you hold sit below it. Members who hold only one pillar see a static label instead of a menu.

## Grant pillars to members

Pillars are granted per member, independently of role. New members start with the Revenue Control Tower and Operations.

- **Settings** > **Team** lets an admin change which pillars each member holds.
- A [join code](/guide/organizations) can carry a set of pillars, so everyone who joins through it starts with the same grants.

Your role (owner, admin, or member) and your pillars combine to decide what you can do. Adding a source, for example, requires the Data pillar at any role. Reprocessing a source requires the Data pillar and an admin or owner role. See [Source card actions](/guide/source-card-actions) for that matrix, and [Ask SyntaxIA](/guide/chat/overview#who-can-do-what) for the settings you can change from the chat.

## Rename a pillar

<Info>
Data, Planning, and Operations are default names. An owner or admin who holds the Data pillar can rename them to match the vocabulary your team uses, for example "GTM" instead of "Operations". Only the label changes. URLs, consoles, and member grants stay the same. See [Rename a pillar](/guide/chat/rename-pillars).
</Info>

The Revenue Control Tower and Finance keep their names. The rest of this documentation uses the default names throughout.
