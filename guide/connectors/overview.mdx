A **connector** is the integration between Syntaxia and one of your source systems. You add connectors from the **Add Source** screen in the Data pillar, and each one becomes a source card on your command center once it is active. The screen shows one list of sources. The same list opens from **Add source** in the Desk's **Sources** panel and from **Add Source** in **Settings**.

Syntaxia uses a managed data integration layer to run every connector, so the steps to add a source are nearly identical across CRMs. You authorize Syntaxia on the source platform, your data lands in our pipeline, and your source card moves from `pending` to `active` once the first sync completes.

You can also add a source without any connector at all. The **Export upload** choice in the **Salesforce** card ingests a Salesforce export zip through the same pipeline as a live connection. See [Upload a CRM export](/guide/connectors/crm-export-upload).

## Supported sources

Syntaxia supports connectors at three levels of maturity. **Available** connectors are fully wired into the golden layer. **Limited** connectors sync data but do not yet map onto the model. **Upcoming** connectors are on the roadmap.

### Available

| Connector | Purpose | Per-org limit |
|---|---|---|
| [Salesforce](/guide/connectors/salesforce) | Enterprise CRM and sales platform | 4 connections |
| [Salesforce Sandbox](/guide/connectors/salesforce-sandbox) | Salesforce sandbox testing environment | Unlimited |
| [HubSpot](/guide/connectors/hubspot) | Inbound marketing, sales, and CRM | Unlimited |
| [Pipedrive](/guide/connectors/pipedrive) | Sales pipeline management | Unlimited |
| [Power BI](/guide/connectors/power-bi) | Business intelligence and reporting | Unlimited |

### No connector? Upload an export

The **Add Source** screen also offers [Upload a CRM export](/guide/connectors/crm-export-upload). You upload a Salesforce export zip, Syntaxia reshapes it into the same pipeline as a live source, and the result is a normal source card with a point-in-time snapshot of the org. There is no OAuth step and no sync schedule: to refresh the data, you re-upload a newer export. Salesforce is the only supported CRM export today.

### ZoomInfo enrichment overlay

ZoomInfo is not a source you connect. When the ZoomInfo managed package enriches a connected Salesforce org, Syntaxia detects the enrichment automatically and creates a linked **ZoomInfo** source alongside the Salesforce parent. The overlay contributes account fields like industry, location, and intent scores to your unified accounts. The Accounts console marks fields that ZoomInfo contributed with a provenance pill. There is nothing to set up and nothing to authorize.

### Limited model support

Data syncs are available, but Syntaxia does not yet map these systems onto the golden layer. You can connect them today and expect mapping coverage in a future release.

- Apollo
- Clari
- Gong
- [Marketo](/guide/connectors/marketo)
- Microsoft Power BI (extended)
- NetSuite
- Salesforce Marketing Cloud

### Upcoming

These connectors are planned but not yet available in the **Add Source** screen.

- 6sense
- Attio
- Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM

## How the connection flow works

Every connector follows the same five-step pattern. The only thing that changes between source systems is which platform you authorize Syntaxia on. The upload path is the exception: it has no authorization step, since there is no platform to authorize against. See [Upload a CRM export](/guide/connectors/crm-export-upload) for that flow.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add Source screen">
    From Source Management in the Data pillar, click **Add Source**. You must hold the Data pillar and be an owner or an admin. Members cannot add sources.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the connector">
    Expand the card for your source in the **Available** list: Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Power BI, or Marketo. Salesforce is one card with three choices: **Production**, **Sandbox**, and **Export upload**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the connection">
    Enter a **Source name** like `Salesforce - Production` or `HubSpot - EMEA`. The name is required and can hold at most 150 characters. Names help you tell sources apart on the command center, especially when an organization runs multiple instances of the same CRM.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize on the source platform">
    Click **Connect**. Syntaxia opens a secure authorization window hosted by our managed pipeline partner. Sign in to the source platform and approve the requested scopes. You will be redirected back to Syntaxia automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the first sync">
    Your source card appears on the command center with status `syncing`. The initial sync usually takes 5 to 30 minutes depending on the size of your CRM. Once complete, the card turns green and shows `active`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If you close the **Add Source** screen or leave the page after you type a source name or select files, Syntaxia asks before it discards them.
</Note>

## Required role

Adding, reconnecting, or removing a source requires the **Data** pillar. Within the Data pillar:

- **Members** can resync, reconnect, and disconnect sources, and re-upload exports on uploaded sources.
- **Admins** and **owners** can additionally add a source, trigger reprocessing, approve mapping corrections, and sign off a source.

If you do not see the **Add Source** button, you do not hold the Data pillar. A member who holds it can open the **Add Source** screen, but each connector there reads "Only admins can add this." in place of its form. See [Pillars](/guide/pillars) and [Source card actions](/guide/source-card-actions) for the full role matrix.

## Per-source limits

Most connectors are unlimited per organization. Salesforce production is capped at **4 live connections** per org. The **Add Source** screen shows the remaining slots next to the **Production** choice in the Salesforce card. When you reach the cap, that choice shows `0 of 4 slots` in place of its form. Disconnecting a source frees a slot only after the source is purged.

Only live connections count toward the cap. An uploaded Salesforce source does not use a slot, and neither does a Salesforce sandbox. The cap never blocks an upload.

## What happens to your data

Once a connector is active, your data flows through the [data pipeline](/guide/data-pipeline). Raw CRM records land in object storage, get normalized into staging tables, are counted, and then surface in the Syntaxia UI. Nothing leaves the pipeline until you ask for it.

When you disconnect a source, the connector is paused and data is preserved. When you purge a source, the connector, the staged data, and the source record are all permanently deleted. See [Source systems](/guide/source-systems) for the full lifecycle.
